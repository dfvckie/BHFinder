Frequently Asked Questions

No. Searching, filtering, and inquiring about properties on BHFinder is 100% free for students, workers, and tenants.
What happens when I click "View Details"?
It opens a direct inquiry modal where you can send your contact details to the verified boarding house owner to schedule an in-person room viewing.
